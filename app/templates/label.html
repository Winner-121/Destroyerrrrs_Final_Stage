Compare the body with its own wheels and the road, not with the photo edges. level = the body sits flat on the wheels. tilted = one side is clearly lower, as if the load were heavier on that side, or the truck is tipping. unclear = the angle does not let you judge, for example a front or rear view.
Look at the width. yes = cargo is wider than the trailer or body, poking out left or right (often seen from the back or a corner). no = cargo stays within the body width. unclear = you cannot see the sides.
Look at the rear end. yes = cargo hangs out behind the trailer end, for example long pipes, timber, a car tail beyond the deck. no = cargo ends at or before the back of the trailer. unclear = the back is not visible.
